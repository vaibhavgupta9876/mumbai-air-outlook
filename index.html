About

This page is an additional submission to the Google Mumbai Data Challenge (Zindi, 2026). It turns the challenge's forecasting task into something a resident can act on: which hours of which days to avoid, and how long the air takes to recover after Diwali.

How to read the outlook

The band is not a confidence interval from a formula. It is the pair of forecasts we actually submit, one scaled low and one scaled high around a five-model average (two Chronos-2 zero-shot runs with weather covariates, a weather-driven gradient-boosting model, and two covariate variants). In the backtests the daily level is the part a six-day forecast gets wrong most, so the band spans the level; the hourly shape inside a day is well predicted.

Why ventilation

PM2.5 in Mumbai is a mixing problem. When the boundary layer is shallow and the wind drops, the same emissions build up to several times the concentration. The ventilation index multiplies the forecast boundary-layer height by the 10 m wind speed; its seasonal terciles give the stagnant, mixed and ventilated labels.

Additional Mumbai history

History_Mumbai_2018_2020.csv.gz: 87,717 rows. CPCB-derived historical
station data (Rohan Rao dataset). Datetime, StationId, StationName and
PM2.5 identify the observations. Other pollutant and AQI columns are
retained. AQI fields are not known future inputs.

History_Mumbai_2024.csv.gz: 216,472 rows. XKDR archive with CPCB/AirNow
series identifiers. collected_at/timestamp_ist are source timestamps
in IST; value is PM2.5 concentration. Averaging intervals have not been
fully aligned to current OpenAQ hour-ending targets.

Legacy_Observations_2020_2022.csv.gz: 12,452 irregular OpenAQ samples.
datetime, location_id, sensors_id and value identify source observations.
These samples are not qualified hourly labels under the current rule.

Other_Mumbai_Stations_2025_2026.csv.gz: 9,125 qualified hourly observations
from other Mumbai stations. mean is PM2.5 concentration, count is the
number of valid unique quarter-hour readings (at least three), and
timestamp_ist marks the hour end. These are auxiliary stations and
are not extra scored test stations. available_at is the retained
source file modification time, not a verified first publication time.

XKDR_All_Stations.csv and Kaggle_All_Stations.csv preserve source station
metadata. They include stations outside Mumbai and are lookup files,
not additional observation datasets. Source IDs from different archives
must not be treated as interchangeable.

Negative or missing PM2.5 values were excluded from prepared hourly
history. Extreme retained values are flagged for review. No automatic
cross-source merging or historical gap filling was performed.

Attribution: CPCB/CAAQMS; OpenAQ; XKDR; Rohan Rao/CPCB-derived station
dataset. Original source terms continue to apply.
